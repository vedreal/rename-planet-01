## The entry

> Venus is now Overtime — its day is longer than its year, so it is always late for its own birthday.

A day on Venus lasts about 243 Earth days, while a year there lasts about
225 — so a Venusian day is longer than a Venusian year. A planet that is
always late for its own birthday.
